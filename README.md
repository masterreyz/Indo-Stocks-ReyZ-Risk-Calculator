# MasterReyz Indo Stocks Risk Calculator

## **About This Tool**

The MasterReyz Indo Stocks Risk Calculator is a specialized risk management tool designed specifically for traders and investors navigating the Indonesian stock market (IDX). This calculator helps you determine precise position sizing and evaluate exact potential trade risks by automatically factoring in local market trading costs.

## **Key Calculation Features**

To ensure highly accurate net profit, loss, and risk-to-reward projections, the calculator strictly integrates the following Indonesian market fee structures:

* **Buy Fee:** Automatically calculates a 0.15% commission on buy transactions.
* **Sell Fee:** Automatically calculates a 0.15% commission on sell transactions.
* **Stamp Duty (Meterai):** Automatically applies the mandatory IDR 10,000 stamp duty to any buy or sell transaction where the gross nominal value exceeds IDR 10,000,000.
